most companies hire for the organisation they were. a few hire for the one they're becoming. this page is for the second kind: courageous enough to describe a role before it exists, ambitious enough to test it in public, and future-oriented enough to want the person who was built for it before anyone else does.

we don't write job descriptions and we don't read CVs. we write missions: a problem worth solving, the trajectory it sits on, and the kind of person who would love solving it. then we find out who that person is.

how it works

four weeks, one mission.

  1. 1
    the question.

    a working session on the one thing your company will need to do that nobody has done. we leave with a problem, not a title.

  2. 2
    the mission.

    we write it as a posting from the future: the company, the problem, the person, the catch. you approve every word.

  3. 3
    the launch.

    it goes on the board and on our channels in your name. people read it, claim a seat, tag the person it was written for.

  4. 4
    the people.

    you meet the ones who were built for it, with the drift as the first conversation. what happens next is yours.
