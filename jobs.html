01 · who we are

you don't know us yet. that's sort of the point.

we come from a little further down the timeline. not far. just far enough that introductions are awkward, our references won't land for another decade, and our LinkedIn says "starting soon".

here's what we can tell you: we are experts in people. we have studied how humans work, why they light up, why they switch off, and exactly how long they can pretend to listen in a meeting. we usually know what you're good at before your CV does.

we came back for one reason. too many brilliant people are spending their lives in jobs that were never designed for them. we'd like to fix that before it becomes history.

so who exactly are we? that will become apparent. sooner than you think. keep watching the sky.

02 · what we do

a launch sequence, not a launch.

impossible jobs isn't one product. it's a series of modules that switch on one at a time, each designed to bring you a little closer to work that actually fits who you are.

the first three are coming online now. after that, we keep going. over time we'll activate more and more products and modules: each one more relevant, more personal and more engaging than the last, and frankly more fun than anything work has offered you so far.

some will help you understand yourself. some will show you futures you didn't know you could want. some will put you to the test. a few we can't describe yet. temporal regulations. you understand.
